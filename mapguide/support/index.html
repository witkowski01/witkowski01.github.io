Reporting an issue

A few details help us understand what happened:

  • Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.
  • The MapGuide version and what you were trying to do.
  • Steps to reproduce the problem and any error message.
  • A screenshot, if useful. Remove private notes and precise locations you do not want to share.

Common questions

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings in MapGuide and export a JSON backup. Save it to a location you control. Use Import to restore a backup; review the confirmation carefully, because restoring replaces the current app data.

Why can’t I find a place or compare a route?

Check your internet connection and try a more specific place name or area. Apple Maps coverage and rate limits may affect results. You can also save a place manually using coordinates.

How do I use my current location?

Choose the current-location option when saving a place or setting your trip start. If permission is denied, review MapGuide’s location permission in iOS Settings. You can still enter a location manually.

How do I delete my data?

Delete lists and trips in the app, then use the option to remove unused places in Settings. Discard any saved import draft in the importer. Deleting the app removes its local data; exported backups remain wherever you saved them.
