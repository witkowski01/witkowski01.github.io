Your lists, notes, and saved trips live on your device. MapGuide has no account system, advertising, or analytics SDK. You choose when to export or share a plan.

Map searches and route comparisons use Apple services. Opening directions shares the necessary route details with the map provider you choose.

FAQ

Before you
head out.

A few things worth knowing about planning with MapGuide.

Does MapGuide work offline?

Saved lists, notes, and trip plans are stored locally. Map search, resolving places, and travel-time comparisons require internet access. MapGuide does not download offline maps.

Can I walk, drive, cycle, or use public transit?

Yes. Choose a travel mode when comparing routes and opening navigation. Availability and estimates depend on your map provider and region. Longer routes may open in stages.

Can I bring in ideas from an AI assistant?

MapGuide can prepare a prompt for you to copy into an AI assistant of your choice. Import the resulting JSON list, review matched places, and confirm what to save. Your saved lists are not sent to an AI service automatically.

Will my plans sync across devices?

There is no cloud sync. You can export a JSON backup and import it on another device. Keep an export before deleting the app: its local data is excluded from automatic device backup.
