First time here?

  1. Install BlueAirShare from your device’s store.
  2. Keep the receiver awake in BlueAirShare, on a reachable local Wi-Fi network.
  3. After installation, open BlueAirShare and scan a still-valid QR again inside the app. Return here if you need these steps.
