BlueAirShare

Share files between your devices over a local connection. File contents are not sent to our servers or to advertising services.

The upcoming iOS and Android editions include a banner advertisement and an optional one-time purchase to remove ads. The purchase belongs to your account in the respective store.

The app is being prepared for release. Store download links will be added when available.
